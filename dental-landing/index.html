Your Journey to a New Smile

From first visit to final crown — we guide you every step of the way.

  1. 01

    Free Consultation & 3D Scan

    We evaluate your bone density with a CBCT 3D scan, review your health history, and create a fully personalized treatment plan — at no charge.

    60 min appointment
  2. 02

    Implant Placement Surgery

    Under local anesthesia or IV sedation, a small titanium post is precisely placed into your jawbone. Most patients describe it as less discomfort than a routine extraction.

    1–2 hr procedure
  3. 03

    Osseointegration (Healing)

    Over 3–6 months, the implant fuses naturally with your bone — a process called osseointegration. A temporary crown keeps you smiling throughout.

    3–6 month healing
  4. 04

    Custom Crown Placement

    Your permanent crown is precision-crafted from ceramic to perfectly match your bite and smile. It's bonded to the abutment and you walk out with your complete new tooth.

    1–2 hr final visit

Common Questions

Everything You're Wondering About

Most patients report less discomfort than a tooth extraction. The area is fully numbed during surgery. Post-procedure soreness typically lasts 2–3 days and is well-managed with over-the-counter pain relievers. IV sedation is available for anxious patients.

Most adults with good general health are candidates. Requirements include adequate jawbone density and healthy gums. Even patients with some bone loss may qualify after a bone graft. Our free CBCT scan will give you a definitive answer within your first visit.

The entire process — from consultation to permanent crown — typically takes 4–8 months. The majority of that time is healing (osseointegration), not active treatment. All-on-4 patients can receive a temporary full arch of teeth on the same day as surgery.

Coverage varies widely. Many plans cover the crown, some cover a portion of the surgery, and some cover nothing. We'll verify your benefits before treatment and identify every dollar of coverage you're entitled to. For uncovered costs, we offer 0% APR financing through CareCredit.

Exactly like a natural tooth — brush twice daily, floss once, and visit us every 6 months for a cleaning. There are no special solutions or adhesives. With proper hygiene, implants can last your entire lifetime.
